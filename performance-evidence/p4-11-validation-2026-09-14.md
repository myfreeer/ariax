## Implementation And Compatibility

Production CLI, transports, Rust handles and shared-call adapters use one
managed control runtime. Queries capture immutable identity-bound roots and
project outside the owner. Two shared query/configuration slots bound execution;
snapshot, request and result-workspace ownership survive cancellation.

Urgent/bulk mailboxes retain profile capacities and bounded-burst fairness.
Coalescing preserves intervening task actions. Bulk targets are captured once;
later explicit task controls supersede unfinished earlier bulk work. Accepted
envelopes and pending replies retain the owner after the last backend disappears.
Shutdown closes admission under the mailbox lock and signals out of band.

Filesystem preparation and configuration validation execute outside the owner.
Import revalidates current queue positions under its publication fence and
installs journals incrementally. Owned session submissions and completion
polling preserve durable publication without blocking owner turns. Productive
turns yield without a timer delay; unready I/O and backpressure retain backoff.
The shared budget stays at 32 steps/about 1 ms, with one bulk target per turn.

Authentication, compatibility response shapes, live authenticated URI queries,
sanitized exports, schema v2, strict journal replay, atomic batch metadata and
durability after disconnect remain covered by the regression suites.

## Native Windows Measurements

All four transport scenarios complete 20,000 measured calls plus 1,000 mutation
verification calls. Each includes 12,000 status calls, 4,000 projections of 128
rows, 1,000 each of file/URI/option queries, and 125 each of eight actual task
mutations. Every ordinary operation's p99 is below 50 ms; worst is 37.543 ms.
The longest burst is 421 ms. Scenarios finish in 43.800–50.213 seconds, below the
unchanged 90-second deadline. Sampled working set stays below 140 MiB; all
RPC/resident limits, active-range barriers and stalled-consumer releases pass.

The administrative scenario completes in 8.988 seconds with zero active ranges.
It imports, exports and saves 128 tasks, checks later intent during bulk resume
and pause, and creates/purges 128 real stopped results. Concurrent query p99 is
at most 1.523 ms and urgent acknowledgement at most 16.631 ms. Shutdown is clean.

The earlier HTTP attempt completed all samples but failed `addUri` p99 at
55.507 ms. It remains marked failed under `supersededRuns` in the raw record.
Ready-turn yielding lowered that operation's HTTP p99 to 30.318 ms; no gate was
relaxed and no incomplete or failed run was counted as acceptance.

## Bounded Fuzz Smoke

The unchanged RPC JSON, session-document and URL-rule parser surfaces each pass
2,000 instrumented runs: four 500-run bursts with 250 ms cooldowns. Individual
bursts take 0.171–0.729 seconds. AddressSanitizer and coverage instrumentation
are enabled; loaded counter counts are 45,471, 12,092 and 39,878 respectively.
These are bounded smoke checks, not long fuzz campaigns or benchmark samples.

`cargo-fuzz` 0.13.2 is installed locally under `toolchains/fuzz-tools`. Builds use
the pinned Linux compiler with `RUSTC_BOOTSTRAP=1`, the default address sanitizer,
16 codegen units and an isolated target directory. Inputs are capped at 8 KiB;
each burst has a 500-run limit, a two-second per-input timeout and a 1 GiB RSS
limit. No global toolchain is changed. The raw record retains burst counts,
elapsed times and coverage summaries.
