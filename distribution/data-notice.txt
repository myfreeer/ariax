Ariax embedded data and source availability

Mozilla Public Suffix List
The exact MPL-2.0-covered data embedded by the retained binaries is included at
source-data/public-suffix-list.dat, with its upstream revision, URL and checksum
in source-data/public-suffix-list.toml. The license is licenses/MPL-2.0.txt.
This covered source is supplied in the same package, at no additional charge.
The data retains MPL-2.0 terms; Ariax-owned application code is licensed under MIT.
Upstream: https://publicsuffix.org/list/public_suffix_list.dat

IANA IPv4 and IPv6 Special-Purpose Address Registries
The data used to derive the destination classifier is included at
source-data/iana-ipv4-special-registry.csv and
source-data/iana-ipv6-special-registry.csv. The source URLs, dates and hashes
are in source-data/iana-special-purpose.pin. Applicable registry rights are
dedicated under CC0-1.0; see licenses/CC0-1.0.txt and the IANA/IETF statement:
https://www.iana.org/help/licensing-terms
The data's CC0 dedication does not grant trademark or patent rights.
Ariax derives a longest-prefix classifier and adds project policy overrides;
it does not alter the supplied pinned CSV snapshots.

Neither upstream organization endorses this application or its policy choices.
